Field Industrial

HIGH-CONTRAST · DAYLIGHT-READABLE · GLOVE-OPERABLE — an evolution of NEPI's current token set, not a departure from it

Principle

NEPI devices are operated outdoors, in vehicles, and on job sites — often through gloves, on a cracked tablet screen, in direct sun. This guide keeps the real app's existing hue family (steel greys, safety amber/green/red) but pushes every edge, border and label toward maximum legibility: thick 2–3px borders instead of hairlines, all-caps monospace labels for anything numeric or state-related, and a paper-white background instead of pure white to cut glare. Nothing here is decorative — every visual choice earns its place by being easier to read at a glance from arm's length.

Rules

— Borders are always 2px or 3px solid ink, never a hairline, never rounded.
— Labels for anything numeric, timestamped, or type-related are set in monospace, uppercase.
— Amber is reserved for the single primary accent per view; green/red are reserved for confirmed-state and danger only, never decoration.
— Background is warm paper (#f3f1ea), not pure white, to reduce glare in direct sun.
— No shadows, no gradients, no border-radius — every edge is a straight, deliberate cut.
Field Industrial extends the color tokens already in Styles.js (grey1/grey2/blue/green/red) rather than replacing them — see UI_mockups/README.md for the full rationale and the concept page that applies this guide to the real Controls Sandbox layout.
