Soft Neumorphic

A tactile, single-surface language where every control looks pressed into or raised out of one continuous background — built for a calm, low-glare indoor or lab console rather than the field.

Principle

Rather than boxes with borders, every element here is the same material

Rules

— Every surface is the same base color; depth comes only from dual-direction soft shadow (light top-left, dark bottom-right).
— Resting/interactive elements are raised; anything read-only or "currently active" is inset.
— Corner radius is generous and consistent: 24px for outer panels, 12–16px for controls, full round for pills/toggles/dots.
— Accent blue is used sparingly — only on the single most important actionable item per section.
— No hard borders anywhere; borders are always implied by shadow, never drawn as a line.
Soft Neumorphic keeps the same 18 demo fields (10 controls + 8 data) and image-connect flow as the real app — see UI_mockups/README.md and concept_2_soft_neumorphic.html for the full layout.
